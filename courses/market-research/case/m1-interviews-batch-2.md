# Buyer interviews, batch 2 (October 2027)

Study: Cold chain monitoring needs, 2027. 6 interviews. Transcript excerpts. Quotes in the excerpts are word for word.

---

## I-109

- Date: 2027-10-06
- Segment: Distribution
- Role: Quality manager, cold storage warehouse (Ridgeway Cold Logistics, 20 freezer rooms)
- Run by: PM-held
- Length: 36 minutes

**Interviewer:** How do your teams react to alerts?

**Buyer:** Slowly. "We had 300 alerts last month and maybe ten were real." The team stopped trusting them.

**Interviewer:** What about audits?

**Buyer:** "Our customer audits need a temperature summary per room, and I build it in a spreadsheet every time."

## I-110

- Date: 2027-10-07
- Segment: Distribution
- Role: Night operations lead, food distributor (Teller Creek Foods, 3 warehouses)
- Run by: AI-moderated
- Length: 24 minutes

**AI moderator:** Who responds to alarms at night?

**Buyer:** Me, or whoever is on call. That is the problem. "I'd pay for a monitoring service that takes the 2 a.m. call and only wakes me if it's real." My managers do not want the phone at night.

**AI moderator:** Anything else about alerts?

**Buyer:** "Half the night alerts are a door left open for a minute during a pick."

## I-111

- Date: 2027-10-08
- Segment: Food service
- Role: Regional manager, restaurant group (Saltgrass Kitchens, 12 sites)
- Run by: PM-held
- Length: 33 minutes

**Interviewer:** How do you see your sites today?

**Buyer:** One at a time. "It would help to see all my sites on one screen, but honestly it's not my top problem." My top problem is the install.

**Interviewer:** Tell me about the install.

**Buyer:** "Three of our walk-ins still drop off the network a few times a week."

## I-112

- Date: 2027-10-09
- Segment: Distribution
- Role: Operations manager, food distributor (Pellham Distribution, 2 warehouses)
- Run by: PM-held
- Length: 40 minutes

**Interviewer:** Who owns night alarms at your sites?

**Buyer:** Officially the supervisors. In practice it is not working. "We tried having supervisors own night alarms, and it failed; we want a service to take that call for us." Two supervisors quit over the night phone this year.

**Interviewer:** What about the sensors themselves?

**Buyer:** "Changing batteries on 200 sensors is a part-time job for someone."

## I-113

- Date: 2027-10-13
- Segment: Pharmacy and lab
- Role: Pharmacy manager, hospital outpatient pharmacy (Fennick Health outpatient pharmacy, 1 site)
- Run by: PM-held
- Length: 31 minutes

**Interviewer:** What do inspectors ask for?

**Buyer:** "Every inspection starts with the calibration certificates, and if one is missing we get a finding."

**Interviewer:** How are the alerts?

**Buyer:** "We get alerts every time the fridge is restocked, so nobody looks anymore."

## I-114

- Date: 2027-10-14
- Segment: Food service
- Role: Store manager, grocery chain (Kestle Market, 6 stores)
- Run by: AI-moderated
- Length: 20 minutes

**AI moderator:** What does a temperature problem cost you?

**Buyer:** "One bad night in the dairy cooler cost us about eleven thousand dollars in product."

**AI moderator:** What about inspections?

**Buyer:** "I print the cooler logs for the inspector every visit, and it takes most of an afternoon."
