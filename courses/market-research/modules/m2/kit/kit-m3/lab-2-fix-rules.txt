# Lab 2 fix rules

The rule a learner adds to "My rules" in the brief after the lab 2 audit. Brief v3 in this kit has it as rule 2.

## Rule 2: deal source rule (in brief v3)

> A deal with no buyer interview is CRM only. Never use the CRM reason or a sales note as a buyer driver. Count only deals with an interview in the match rate table.

Why: a deal where the buyer did not attend, or declined, has no buyer voice. A sales note copied into the interview file is still a CRM reason.

On your own work: keep the rule. Add the deals with no interview to your next interview list.
