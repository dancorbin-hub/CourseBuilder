# PM needs: Advanced AI for Market Research (track 3)

- Course: market-research
- Researcher: Stage 1, track 3 (PM needs)
- Date: 2026-11-20
- Question: what do PMs do in market research, how often, and where does the time go?
- Scope: Market category (discovery and validation). Also Market Definition (Focus category) for market sizing.

## How to read this file

1. Source ids are `N<n>`. The ledger is at the end of the file.
2. Id N14 is not used (its source, Klue, was dropped).
3. "Weak" means a vendor source, an unknown sample or an unclear method. Do not put a weak statistic on a slide.
4. Statistics and studies from before 2026 are dropped. The dropped list is at the end.
5. Practitioner articles older than 12 months have `age_flag: true`. Dan decides if they stay.
6. Limit of this run: the shared web fetch budget ran out near the end. Four pages were found but not opened (see "Not opened" in the ledger).

## Summary in 6 points

1. PMs say they do not get enough time with customers. 71% of 677 product people say they do not spend enough time with customers or on the market (N5).
2. Unplanned work takes the time. About 60% often do unplanned work, and only 25% spend most of their time on strategy (N5).
3. Demand for research grows faster than the people to do it. 70% see more research requests, and 57% lack the resources (N10, market research staff, not only PMs).
4. AI moved the work. 80% of senior product leaders use AI to run or synthesize research (N7). But 71% of research practitioners say checking AI output still takes much time (N11).
5. The slow steps before the interview (finding, recruiting and scheduling people) are a main pain. Pragmatic names 8 barriers to market visits (N2). The only figure is from a vendor (N16).
6. Hardware and manufacturing teams have much feedback but turn it into decisions slowly. Speed is the biggest obstacle (45%, N17, weak). Internal PM has no 2026 source.
