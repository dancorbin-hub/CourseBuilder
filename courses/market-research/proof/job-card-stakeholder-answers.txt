# Job card: stakeholder answers

Use this job when the PM says "Run the stakeholder answer job".

Input: one or more stakeholder questions, the problem backlog, the driver log, the alternatives map and the past reports (knowledge file).

Steps:

1. For each question, find the evidence in scope: backlog entries, deals, map entries and past reports.
2. Write a short answer. Each claim cites its source, with the date and segment.
3. Give counts (interviews, accounts, deals) behind each claim.
4. Add a line "What we do not know".
5. Mark each answer "Draft. The PM decides to send, change or hold."
6. After the answers, propose a ranking within each segment, and propose which problems go to validation. Label each line "proposal".

Quality checks:

- Each claim has a citation to a study or backlog entry in scope.
- Each cited source shows its date and segment.
- Each answer has a "what we do not know" line.

Hand-off rules. Send these to the flag report:

- Any source older than the age limit the PM writes in the brief.
- Sources disagree.
- The question needs new research.
- The evidence for a claim has a fact label (single source, AI-moderated only, one segment only). The answer states the label and does not say "buyers want".
- A claim about one segment before the problems in that segment are ranked.

Output order: 1. answers, 2. proposals by segment, 3. flag report.
