# Win/loss interview notes, Q2 2026

Interviews run by the product team or an outside firm. Sales did not run any interview. Quotes are word for word from the recordings. Buyers agreed to the interview and to confidential use.

---

## WL-2201 (deal D-2201, Harlow Bay Foods, won)

- Interview date: 2026-04-27. Run by: Product team
- Buyer role: Quality manager

We chose Larkspan for the reports. "Your audit report was the only one that matched what our customers' auditors ask for, line for line." The trial was smooth. Price was in the middle of the three quotes.

---

## WL-2202 (deal D-2202, Copperleaf Kitchens, won)

- Interview date: 2026-05-06. Run by: Outside firm
- Buyer role: Director of operations

Price was close between the two. The install decided it. "The contractor had all six sites live in one visit each, and Vantafrost wanted a separate installer." We already trust that contractor with our refrigeration.

---

## WL-2203 (deal D-2203, Stonemere Cold Storage, lost)

- Interview date: 2026-05-11. Run by: Product team
- Buyer role: Operations manager

We ran both pilots in the same freezer rooms. "Your hub kept dropping the sensors in our two blast freezers, and Rimecore's didn't drop once in three weeks." Price was a little higher for Rimecore. It did not matter after the pilot.

---

## WL-2205 (deal D-2205, Juniper Yard Cafes, lost)

- Interview date: 2026-05-26. Run by: Outside firm
- Buyer role: Owner and operator

We liked the product. The subscription was the issue for four small cafes. "Four site subscriptions was more than we spend on the coolers' service contract, so we stayed with paper logs." We may look again next year.

---

## WL-2206 (deal D-2206, Ashcombe Lab Services, won)

- Interview date: 2026-06-01. Run by: Product team
- Buyer role: Lab manager

Calibration was the reason. "You include the calibration certificates with the probe serial number on them, so my auditor stops asking." Vantafrost wanted us to send probes out for calibration.

## WL-2207 (deal D-2207, Breckwood Distribution, lost)

- Interview date: 2026-06-09. Run by: Product team
- Buyer role: VP operations

Sales thinks we left for the multi-site dashboard. That was nice to have. The trial is what lost it. "We got over forty alerts in the first week of the trial, and most were dock doors during loading." My team voted for Rimecore after that week.

## WL-2209 (deal D-2209, Ferrow Street Grill Group, lost)
