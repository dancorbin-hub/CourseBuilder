# Lab 5 rules

The rules a learner adds to "My rules" in the brief in lab 5. Brief v6 in this kit has them as rules 5 and 6.

## Rule 5: age limit and what the agent may say (step 2, in brief v6)

> Age limit: 18 months. Send any source older than 18 months to the flag report. Every answer is a draft for me. Name no person in an answer. Make no commitment for the team: no roadmap item, date or price.

The 18 months is the case PM's choice. On your own work, set your own age limit.

## Rule 6: the citation audit rule (debrief, in brief v6)

> Do not use a source for a segment it did not study. Flag any source older than my age limit. Rank within a segment before any claim about it. Never write '<segment> buyers want' from 1 interview in that segment. Write what we know, with counts, dates and segments, and what we do not know.

Why: a citation can be real and still not support the answer. A flag for an old source does not stop the answer text from saying more than the source. Open every citation before you send.

On your own work: keep the rule. Ask the agent your last stakeholder question. Open every citation before you send the answer.
