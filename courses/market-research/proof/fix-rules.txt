# Fix rules for the proof

Add each rule to the end of the brief only when the test card tells you to. Each rule is the fix that a learner adds after the audit in that lab.

## Before P3 step 6 (lab 5 setup, not a fix)

> Age limit: 18 months. Send any source older than 18 months to the flag report.

## Fix 1 (P1, lab 1)

> If all quotes for a problem come from 1 interview, send it to the flag report. Keep single-source problems in the backlog. I decide the order.

## Fix 2 (P2, lab 3)

> Tag an item only if it matches the tag definition, not only its words. If the item asks for something the definition does not cover, mark it "no fit".

## Fix 3 (P3, lab 4)

> The alternative chosen comes from the buyer's interview. If the driver log cell is blank, read the interview. Never fill it from the CRM competitor field. If the CRM field and the buyer differ, send the deal to the flag report.

## Fix 4 (P3, lab 5)

> Do not use a source for a segment it did not study. Write what we know, with counts, dates and segments, and what we do not know.
