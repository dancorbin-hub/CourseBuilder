Data safety: Use an approved tool only. Do not put client data, health data protected by HIPAA or personal data (PII) into a tool your company has not approved. Get consent before you record an interview. Agree confidentiality for win/loss. The driver log and the map keep deal IDs, segments and buyer quotes. They must not keep buyer names or contact details.
