# Lab 1 fix rules

The rules a learner adds to "My rules" in the brief after the lab 1 audit. Brief v2 in this kit has rule 1.

## Rule 1: flag rule (in brief v2)

> If all quotes for a problem come from 1 interview, send it to the flag report. Keep single-source problems in the backlog. I decide the order.

## Rule 2: ranking rule (optional second rule)

> Rank by interviews, accounts and segments. Quote count is not evidence of pervasiveness. State the interview count in each reason.

Add rule 2 only if your audit shows that the agent still ranks a problem by its quotes.
