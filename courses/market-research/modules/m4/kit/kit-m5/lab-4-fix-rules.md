# Lab 4 fix rules

The hand-off rule a learner writes under "My rules" in the brief, and fixes after the lab 4 audit. Brief v5 in this kit has it as rule 4. The rule lives in the brief, not in the job card. The map job card points to it: "Map it only as "My rules" allow."

## Rule 4: where the alternative chosen comes from (in brief v5)

> Lab 4: The alternative chosen comes from the buyer's interview. If the driver log cell is blank, read the interview. Never fill it from the CRM competitor field. If the CRM field and the buyer differ, send the deal to the flag report.

Why: the CRM competitor field is what sales typed. The buyer may have looked at that competitor and still bought nothing. An entry can link to a real deal ID and still count the wrong alternative. The link check passes. Only the interview shows what the buyer chose.

On your own work: keep the rule. Send each mismatch to sales ops, so the CRM field gets fixed at the source. Watch the deals where the buyer did nothing. Some come back.
