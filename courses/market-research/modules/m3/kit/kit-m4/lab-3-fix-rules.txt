# Lab 3 fix rules

The rule a learner adds to "My rules" in the brief after the lab 3 audit. Brief v4 in this kit has it as rule 3.

## Rule 3: "no fit" rule (in brief v4)

> Tag an item only if it matches the tag definition, not only its words. If the item asks for something the definition does not cover, mark it no fit.

Why: an item can share words with a tag and still ask for something else. A reason that quotes the item can pass the reason check and still be the wrong tag. A "no fit" answer is better than a guess.

On your own work: keep the rule. Read the "no fit" group every week. A new need often shows up there first.
