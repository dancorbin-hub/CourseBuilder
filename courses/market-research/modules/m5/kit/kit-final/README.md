## On your own work

1. Change the [EDIT] line in the brief: your product, market and segments.
2. Change the age limit in rule 5 to your own.
3. Replace the case knowledge files with your own code book, drivers, tags, map and past reports. Keep the formats.
4. Start the logs empty. Use the empty `problem-backlog.md` in `kit-m1-template`. The win/loss job starts with no driver log.
5. Keep "My rules". Add a rule after each audit.

## Copilot with no license or billing

Paste `copilot-fallback-instructions-m5-v6.txt` into **Instructions** to run the stakeholder answer job. Paste the logs and past reports in chat, as on the lab 5 card. To run another job, use that module's fallback text: `kit-m2` (job 1), `kit-m3` (job 2), `kit-m4` (job 3), `kit-m5` (job 4). Those texts hold an older brief. Add your newer rules under "My rules".

Data safety: Use an approved tool only. Do not put client data, health data protected by HIPAA or personal data (PII) into a tool your company has not approved. Get consent before you record an interview. Agree confidentiality for win/loss. An agent that keeps a backlog stores data longer than a one-off prompt. Decide what it may keep and for how long.
