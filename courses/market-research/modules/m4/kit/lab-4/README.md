## Copilot with no license or billing

Your agent cannot read knowledge files.

1. Paste all of `copilot-fallback-instructions-m4.txt` into **Instructions**. It replaces the module 3 text. Select **Update**.
2. At step 3, send the evidence pack in one message. Upload it, or paste the text (15,350 characters). If the paste fails, send it in 2 parts. Split it at "## Part 3".
3. Debrief rerun: in the new chat, send the evidence pack again.
4. Keep the map and the driver log from the debrief rerun on your computer. Paste them in chat at the next run.

The text holds brief v4 with the lab 1 to lab 3 rules from the kit. If your own rules differ, put yours under "My rules". The fallback agent does one job at a time. To rerun the module 3 job, use `kit-m4/copilot-fallback-instructions-m3-v4.txt`.

Data safety: Use an approved tool only. Do not put client data, health data protected by HIPAA or personal data (PII) into a tool your company has not approved. Get consent before you record an interview. Agree confidentiality for win/loss. Deal records hold customer names, deal values and competitor prices. Keep them only in an approved tool.
