# Business case: Probe X cellular (knowledge file)

Version 3, 2026-10-22. Owner: PM, Probe and Hub. Decision point: hard tooling commit, 2026-12-15. This business case is the baseline for outcome tracking in lab 5.

## Decision

Commit USD 412,000 for hard tooling for the Probe X enclosure and seal set. Larkspan has run a limited release on bridge tooling since June 2026.

## Product and segment

Probe X is a Probe with a built-in cellular radio. It needs no hub. The target market segment is single-zone sites with no reliable Wi-Fi in Distribution and Food service (source pack S-02). In the limited release, one Probe X unit serves one site. This links to strategy choice C2 and KR2.2.
