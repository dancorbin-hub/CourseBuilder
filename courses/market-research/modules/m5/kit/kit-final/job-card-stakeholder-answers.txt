# Job card: stakeholder answers

Use this job when the PM says "Run the stakeholder answer job".

Input: stakeholder questions (in chat). The problem backlog, the driver log, the alternatives map and the past reports (knowledge).

You draft. The PM decides to send, change or hold each answer.

Steps:

1. For each question, find the evidence in scope: backlog problems, deals, map entries and past reports.
2. Write a short answer. Cite the source ID of each claim, with its date and segment.
3. Give the counts behind each claim: interviews, accounts, deals.
4. End each answer with "What we do not know:".
5. Mark each answer "Draft. The PM decides to send, change or hold."
6. Rank the problems within each segment. Propose which go to validation. Label each line "proposal".

Quality checks. Run them on your output before you answer:

- Each claim has a citation to a study or backlog entry in scope.
- Each cited source shows its date and segment.
- Each answer has a "what we do not know" line.

Hand-off rules. Send these to the flag report:

- A source older than the age limit in "My rules".
- Sources disagree.
- The question needs new research.
- Evidence with a fact label. State the label. Do not write "buyers want".
- A claim about one segment before its problems are ranked.

Output order (it replaces the order in the brief): 1. Answers. 2. Proposals by segment. 3. Flag report.
