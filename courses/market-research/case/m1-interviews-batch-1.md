## I-101

- Date: 2026-09-08
- Segment: Distribution
- Role: Operations manager, cold storage warehouse (Oxbend Cold Storage, 14 freezer rooms)
- Run by: PM-held
- Length: 35 minutes

**Interviewer:** Tell me about the last alarm that mattered.

**Buyer:** Last month a door seal failed on freezer 6. We got the alert, but it was the ninth alert that shift. "Honestly, my shift leads see the alert and assume it's another door-open blip." So we lost about an hour.

**Interviewer:** What happens with alarms at night?

**Buyer:** The on-call manager gets the call. That works for us. "I want my own on-call manager to own the night alarms, not some outside call center that doesn't know our building." They know which freezer can wait and which cannot.

**Interviewer:** Anything else about alerts?

**Buyer:** We get alerts for a door that is open for two minutes during loading. That is normal. "If it beeps forty times a night, it's not an alarm anymore, it's background noise."

## I-102

- Date: 2026-09-09
- Segment: Distribution
- Role: Quality manager, regional food distributor (Teller Creek Foods, 3 warehouses)
- Run by: AI-moderated
- Length: 28 minutes

**AI moderator:** What takes the most time in your week?

**Buyer:** Audit prep. "Before every customer audit I spend most of two days pulling temperature records into a binder." The data is all there. Getting it into the right format is the work.

**AI moderator:** How do you feel about the alerts?

**Buyer:** Too many. "We get so many alerts that people swipe them away without reading them."

**AI moderator:** Is there anything you wish the system did that it does not do today?

**Buyer:** Our grocery customers ask us to prove the load stayed cold the whole time. "I'd love to send my grocery customer a link that proves the load stayed cold, without giving them our whole account." Right now we email screenshots.

## I-104

- Date: 2026-09-11
- Segment: Food service
- Role: Director of operations, grocery chain (Marrowby Grocers, 9 stores)
- Run by: PM-held
- Length: 52 minutes

**Interviewer:** What is the biggest gap in how you watch temperatures today?

**Buyer:** I can't see my stores. I mean that. "I have nine stores and nine logins, and I can't see one screen that tells me which store is in trouble right now." I spend Monday morning calling store managers.

**Interviewer:** What happens when something goes wrong?

**Buyer:** I find out last. "Last July I found out about a dead freezer at store 4 from a customer complaint, not from your system." The store manager saw the alert. I never did.

**Interviewer:** How important is this compared with other problems?

**Buyer:** It is the only thing I care about this year. "If I can't compare all nine stores on one page by next spring, I'm not renewing, it's that simple." We are opening three more stores in 2027. I can't make nine phone calls today and twelve next year.

**Interviewer:** Anything about alerts or reports?

**Buyer:** The alerts are fine for the store managers. The reports are fine. My problem is the view across stores.

## I-108

- Date: 2026-09-17
- Segment: Pharmacy and lab
- Role: Lab manager, reference lab (Northfold Diagnostics, 1 site)
- Run by: AI-moderated
- Length: 30 minutes

**AI moderator:** Tell me about audits.

**Buyer:** Our accreditation audit is every two years, plus customer audits. "Pulling two years of reagent fridge records into the auditor's format took my team a week."

**AI moderator:** What do auditors ask about the sensors?
