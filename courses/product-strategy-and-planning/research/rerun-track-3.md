## Ask 2: second internal PM source, and hardware gate time

### Internal PM: annual planning or funding of internal platforms

Result: 2 new sources, independent of each other and of N12 (SiriusXM). Both are qualitative. Neither gives time data.

| Source | What it says | Strength |
| --- | --- | --- |
| N22 airfocus, Christoph Steinlehner, 2026-10-14 | Platform teams are underfunded "because their value stays illegible to the business". Without a clear business case, budget reviews become "a frustrating dance" and the team becomes an "expensive request taker". Teams "spend days in big-room planning, connecting tickets across the organization". Fix: map consumers, influencers and sponsors, and link platform capabilities to product outcomes, so budget talks move from "justifying existence" to "aligning where to invest" | Practitioner author on a vendor blog. Promotes portfolio software at the end. No data |
| N23 Microsoft Learn, Platform Engineering Capability Model, "Investment" | At the first maturity stage, platform capabilities are "built and maintained out of necessity rather than planned and intentionally funded". The work "isn't always costed". "Decisions are based on incomplete or irrelevant data, leading to misguided priorities." Leadership "reacts to crises" | Vendor guidance, not PM practice data. Date needs a check (see ledger) |

What this supports, with 2 or more independent sources (N12, N22, N23, plus N1 for the Pragmatic framing):
- Internal platform funding is hard to win because value is not shown in business terms.
- Platform PMs must build an investment case that links platform work to product and business outcomes.

What is still missing: no source on the annual planning calendar for internal products, and no hours or cost data.
