## Dan's words (summary, identifying details removed)

- Client: a very large global software and business consulting company. Do not name it.
- Pragmatic had a long relationship with the client.
- The client was so large that it decided to build its own internal training system: a training center of excellence.
- Setting it up took a long time. The client worked to set norms.
- The client found it was still hard to meet the needs of very different teams.
- The client came back to Pragmatic. It asked for private classes for those teams, and for Pragmatic to tailor its core classes and AI classes to them. Pragmatic signed a new contract and works with them now.
- In the end, a mix of both (internal center plus Pragmatic classes) worked best. Much of the client's product operations rests on principles and practices Pragmatic first taught them.
